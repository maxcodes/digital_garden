_Updated September 2nd, 2026, from the Galápagos Islands_

## Galápagos with my princesita

I'm in the Galápagos with my princesita—my Tita—and we are doing a very good
job of enjoying life.

We just went diving and saw a hammerhead shark, sea turtles, manta rays, bright
yellow starfish, an orange fish with one little horn, and even a sea snake.

The wildlife doesn't stop at the water: there are iguanas all over the beach,
and at Tortuga Bay a turtle managed to scare us. Fair enough. This is their
place.

> "Don't come to Galapagos if you don't want to wake up at 6:00 AM, it's a
> trap."
>
> — Tita

## Work

Working on [Recurrente.com](https://recurrente.com), a payments platform based
in Guatemala with the mission to bring economic prosperity to the region.

**AI agents & agentic engineering:** I'm still excited about what the future
holds in agentic engineering. I feel like we're exploring how to restructure a
software engineering org from first principles: where AI is useful, where human
judgment matters most, how to avoid fatigue, and how to keep powerful tools in
check. Codex is my main coding tool right now.

The practical question is becoming more interesting than the abstract one: how
do you use agents to ship better products without losing taste, context, or the
habit of listening to users?
